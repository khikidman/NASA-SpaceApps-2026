Recommended lead: B's interaction model within A's world; use C as a permanent
learning journal. Do not combine all three screen shells.

Targeted edits give B/C an astronaut readiness meter for Energy; C's forecast
also uses it, and B's character now has the established navy visor. Originals
remain beside the v2 files; exact edit prompts are in refinement-prompts.json.

Before production, reduce ornamental frames, preserve the existing astronaut
identity, make B's small forecast Energy icon match the readiness meter too, and
ensure cost/gain signs remain paired with red/green plus plain-language labels.
The illustrations approximate typography and interaction
states; they are not proof of responsive behavior or scientific accuracy.

The physical miniature is an authored teaching model. A check mark represents
the game's simplified check, not a claim that a single visible filter makes
lunar water potable. Final equipment should communicate treatment AND testing,
with the accurate real-space-station fact and optional supporting source.

The diagrams and giant facts are intentionally high priority in response to the
user's latest direction; learning is no longer relegated to optional notes.
Do not assign a perfect professional score from concept art. Apply the player,
resource-literacy, accessibility and educator-review criteria in README.md.
